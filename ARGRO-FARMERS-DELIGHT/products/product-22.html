Professional Knapsack Sprayer 20L

Heavy-duty professional-grade knapsack sprayer with 20L capacity, brass pump, and adjustable nozzles—ideal for larger plot protection and fungicide/insecticide application.

Capacity 20 liters
Pump Brass piston pump (high-pressure, durable)
Pressure Up to 80 PSI (good for fungicide/insecticide coverage)
Nozzles 3 interchangeable types (straight, fan, mist)
Hose Length 3.5m flexible hose with wand

What's Included

  • 20L poly tank with shoulder straps and waist belt
  • Brass piston pump with manual pressure gauge
  • 3 interchangeable spray nozzles
  • Strainers (in-tank and on wand)
  • Safety relief valve
  • Spare seals and O-rings kit
  • User manual with maintenance tips

Applications

  • Pesticide and insecticide application on vegetables, cereals, and fruits
  • Fungicide spraying for disease management
  • Herbicide spot treatment (with caution and separate dedicated sprayer)
  • Fertilizer and foliar feeding

Maintenance & Safety

  • Clean thoroughly after each use to prevent pump corrosion
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from children
  • Always wear PPE: gloves, long sleeves, boots, mask, and goggles
