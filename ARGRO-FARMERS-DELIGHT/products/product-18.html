Organic Fungicide Solution

₦8,500

Sulfur-based powder formulation, organic-certified and effective for powdery mildew, leaf spot, and other fungal diseases on vegetables and fruits.

Type Sulfur-based fungicide powder (80% sulfur)
Certification Organic-approved
Target Crops Tomatoes, peppers, cucurbits, leafy greens, fruits
Efficacy Preventive and curative; apply at first sign of disease

Application Guide

  • Mix at 2–3 kg per 100L water or follow label instructions
  • Apply early morning or late afternoon (avoid high heat)
  • Reapply every 7–10 days or after rain
  • Always wear PPE: gloves, mask, and eye protection
  • Follow local regulations and label recommendations
