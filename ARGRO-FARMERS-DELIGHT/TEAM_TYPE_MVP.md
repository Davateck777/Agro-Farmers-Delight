



For a **solo** build with **15 products**, you want a small set of page **types** that scales without duplication. A practical suggestion:

### Must-have page types (MVP)
1. **Home** — positioning for agriculture, trust strip, 3–6 featured products, links to categories and best “Buy on Selar” CTAs.  
2. **Category listing** — one page per category (e.g. Seeds, Crop protection, Tools, Services) showing **cards** for products in that category. With 15 products, **3–5 categories** is enough.  
3. **Product detail** — one page per product: story/specs, images, FAQ-ish bullets, **single primary CTA** to Selar (and optional secondary: contact).  
4. **All products (catalog)** — optional but useful: one grid/list of all 15 with **client-side filter** (category/tag) so nothing feels “lost.”  

So your **minimum set** is often: **Home + Category(ies) + Product detail**. Catalog can replace multiple category pages if you prefer one hub + filters.

### Strongly recommended (still lightweight)
5. **About / Mission** — why you curate for farmers; builds trust before redirecting to Selar.  
6. **Contact / Support** — email, WhatsApp, phone; how to get help if Selar checkout has issues.  
7. **Policies** — short refund/shipping note (“fulfilled per Selar seller terms”) + agronomic disclaimer if you sell inputs.  

### Nice-to-have (only if it stays static)
8. **Season / “What to plant now”** — editorial page you update monthly (no CMS needed).  
9. **404** — friendly “page not found” with links home + catalog.  

---

### Concrete recommendation for **15 products + solo**
Use **two listing strategies** (pick one):

| Strategy | Page types | When it fits |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| **A. Category-led** | Home + 3–5 **Category** pages + **15 Product** pages + About + Contact | Clear shopping mental model; more pages but each is simple. |
| **B. Catalog-led** | Home + **1 Catalog** page (filters) + **15 Product** pages + About + Contact | Fewer listing pages; a bit more JS. |

Either way, **15 product detail pages** is the right shape for SEO and sharing individual Selar links.

---

### Page count sanity check (excluding legal variants)
- **~21–25 URLs** is typical for Strategy A (home + 4 categories + 15 products + about + contact + 404).  
- **~20–22 URLs** for Strategy B (home + catalog + 15 products + about + contact + 404).
