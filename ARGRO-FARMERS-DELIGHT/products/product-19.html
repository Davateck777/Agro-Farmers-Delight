Biological Pest Control Kit

₦16,200

Complete integrated pest management solution using beneficial nematodes and parasitic wasps to naturally control soil and above-ground pests.

Contents Beneficial nematodes (Steinernema carpocapsae) + parasitic wasps (Trichogramma)
Target Pests Root-feeding insects, caterpillars, armyworms, whiteflies
Application Soil drench + foliar spray (follow instructions)
Coverage Suitable for 50–100 sq meters plot

What's Included

  • Beneficial nematode culture pack (ready to apply)
  • Parasitic wasp cards for release
  • Detailed application and storage guide
  • Best timing and temperature recommendations

Best Practices

  • Apply when soil moisture is adequate
  • Release wasps at dusk to prevent UV damage
  • Reduce or avoid broad-spectrum pesticides during application
  • Reapply every 4–6 weeks for continuous protection
