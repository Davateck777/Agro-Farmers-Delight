Advanced Soil Analysis & Nutrient Report

₦55,000

Professional laboratory soil testing service that provides comprehensive macronutrient and micronutrient analysis with detailed fertilizer recommendations tailored to your plot and crop type.

Turnaround 5–7 working days from sample receipt
Report Format Digital PDF + hardcopy (optional)
Coverage Recommended for plots up to 2 hectares
Includes Macronutrients, micronutrients, organic matter, pH, and fertilizer rates

What's Tested

  • Macronutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K)
  • Secondary Nutrients: Calcium (Ca), Magnesium (Mg), Sulfur (S)
  • Micronutrients: Iron (Fe), Zinc (Zn), Boron (B), Manganese (Mn), Copper (Cu)
  • Organic Matter: Percentage and quality assessment
  • pH & Buffering: Acidity/alkalinity and lime requirements

What You'll Receive

  • Detailed lab report with test results and nutrient levels
  • Fertilizer recommendations (rates, timing, and application methods)
  • Lime or sulfur requirement (if soil amendment needed)
  • Suitability assessment for intended crop
  • Optional follow-up consultation with agronomist (by email or video)

How to Order

  1. Purchase this service on Selar
  2. Receive sample collection kit (bags, forms, instructions)
  3. Collect soil samples from 5–10 spots across your plot (mix well)
  4. Send labeled samples to our partner lab
  5. Receive full report within 5–7 days
  6. Implement recommendations and track yield changes
